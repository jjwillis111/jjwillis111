Why we built this

Built for quick sharing

Everything happens in three steps. No user accounts, no passwords, nothing to remember.

1

Pick the spot

Click any restaurant on the map (or the sidewalk right outside of it) to capture the location.

2

Describe the deal

Add the key details: what the offer is, price point, and when it ends.

3

Publish instantly

Your marker appears for everyone along with an accessible card in the feed below the map.

Privacy-first approach

We only store the details required to show your pin: restaurant name, description, and the coordinates you selected. Everything is saved locally in your browser so you can edit or clear it anytime.
